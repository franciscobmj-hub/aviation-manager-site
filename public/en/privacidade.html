Privacy Policy

Initial template for legal and technical review before public launch.

1. Data processed

Depending on platform configuration, processed data may include registration data, operational information, flight records, notification recipients and data required for integrations authorized by the user.

2. Purposes

Data is used to provide contracted functionality, authenticate users, record operations, generate reports and execute configured integrations.

3. Third-party services

Integrations with Google and Meta/WhatsApp are also subject to the respective providers' policies.

4. Security

Sensitive credentials must remain in the backend, and the production application should use HTTPS and appropriate access controls.

5. Required review

Before commercial publication, this policy should be reviewed to reflect the final architecture, applicable legal basis, retention periods and the controller's official contact channels.
